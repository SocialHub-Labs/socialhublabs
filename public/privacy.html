PRIVACY INFORMATION · 8 OCT 2026

Privacy, simply.

SocialHub Labs is an early-stage independent software project. The current products — Link Page Studio, Social Card Maker and Campaign Link Builder — run primarily in your browser.

What the tools save

SocialHub Studio saves profile drafts using your browser's localStorage. They are not uploaded to a SocialHub account or database. You can reset that data in Studio or clear the site's local storage in your browser settings.

Shared preview URLs

When you choose “Copy preview link”, the profile content is encoded into the link's URL fragment (the part after #). Your browser uses that content to render the preview. Fragments are not normally included in HTTP requests to the origin server, but anyone who receives the link can read the profile and it may remain in browser history, chats or link previews. Do not put private data in a profile intended for sharing.

Generated files

HTML pages, JSON backups, campaign URLs and PNG images are generated in your browser. The current tools do not need user accounts, analytics SDKs, cookies for advertising, or remote third-party APIs.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most web hosting services, Cloudflare processes requests, including network metadata such as IP addresses, and may retain technical/security logs according to its policies. This statement is not a promise of complete anonymity.

External links

Links that you or other people add to a profile can point to third-party sites, which have their own privacy practices. The tools do not verify or endorse linked websites.

Contact

For questions, email hello@socialhub.win.

Update this information if you later add analytics, a hosted database, accounts, cookies, third-party services or new data handling.
